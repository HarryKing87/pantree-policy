Legal · Data & Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Mål collects, uses, and protects your information — written plainly so you always know what's on the shelf.

Last updated 20 February 2025
Welcome to Mål! Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our app. By using Mål, you agree to the practices described below.
01

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information — when you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and other necessary details.
  • Nutrition & health data — if you log meals or receive dietary recommendations, we may collect data related to your preferences, goals, and health.
  • Pantry data — information about the food items you store in your virtual pantry, including expiry dates.
  • Usage information — data on how you interact with the app, such as features used and time spent.
  • Device information — data related to your device, operating system, and IP address, for security and optimization.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve Mål's features, including AI-powered meal planning and dietary recommendations.
  • Offer personalized nutrition insights and pantry management.
  • Connect you with real-life nutritionists, if applicable.
  • Enhance user experience and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
03

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. However, we may share it with:

  • Nutritionists & experts — if you opt for professional consultations.
  • Third-party service providers — to facilitate app functionalities like data storage and analytics.
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect against fraudulent activity.
04

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no digital system is completely secure.

05

Your Rights & Choices

You can:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of certain data collection practices.
Questions or requests about your data? Reach us at info@pantryapp.com
06

Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your Mål account and associated personal data at any time. Once we receive your request, we will delete your account information, including your profile details, pantry contents, and nutrition data, within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain data for legal or regulatory purposes.

To request deletion, send an email with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your account.

Request deletion by emailing info@pantryapp.com See the full deletion steps →
07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app, and continued use of Mål means you accept the revised policy.
