Updates · What changed

Release Notes

Every update to Mål, explained plainly — what changed and why it matters.

Sep 19, 2026 v0.5.6 · Build 46 Stability Drop

No more blank screens.

A tester caught a nasty setup bug before you ever could — now it's fixed, along with a few other rough edges, and we've added the tools to catch the next one even faster. 🛡️

🔢
Setup numbers, corrected
Weight, height, and age entered during setup could end up showing the wrong number in Account Details afterward. What you enter is now exactly what gets saved.
🚪
No more blank screens after sign-up Fix
A routing hiccup could leave new accounts stuck on an empty tab bar right after registering. Completing sign-up now reliably lands you in the app.
🍽️
Favorite cuisines, now visible
The cuisines you picked during setup were saved all along — they just weren't shown anywhere. Now they appear in Account Details.
🔍
Crash reporting, added
We added the tools to catch and fix problems faster, often before you'd even notice them.
🛡️
Security tune-up
A few under-the-hood dependency and configuration issues were cleaned up, keeping your data and account safer.
🔄
Profile loading, more resilient
If your profile data failed to load the first time, the app now automatically retries a few times before giving up — so a flaky connection is less likely to leave you stuck.
Sep 8, 2026 v0.5.5 · Build 45 Shopping List Drop

Lists that actually work.

A cluster of shopping list fixes — items stay where you put them, duplicates are handled gracefully, and your pantry finally talks to your meal plan. 🛒

🗂️
Category changes now save correctly Fix
Moving a shopping list item to a different category would silently fail. The new category now persists immediately and survives a refresh.
🍳
Smarter pantry duplicates
Adding an item you already have in your pantry now shows a choice: merge the quantities into the existing entry, or keep them separate. No more silent rejections.
🧠
Pantry-aware shopping lists
AI-generated shopping lists now check your pantry first. If you already have enough of an ingredient, it won't appear on the list — only genuine shortfalls make the cut.
🌍
Translation improvements
Several missing labels in the shopping list flow — including loading and saving states — are now fully translated across all six supported languages.
Aug 28, 2026 v0.5.2 · Build 42 Health Export

Your data, your doctor.

Export your calorie log, macros, and weight history as a branded PDF or CSV — ready to share with your care team.

📤
Export your health data
A new "Take your data further" card on the dashboard opens an export modal. Pick 7, 15, or 30 days, choose CSV or PDF, and share via the native share sheet.
🎯
Honest macro numbers
Macros reflect only what you actually ate. Instant-mode users see logged day totals; meal-plan users see per-meal sums from marked meals only.
📄
Branded PDF report
The PDF shows a profile card, three stat cards (avg kcal, adherence, weight change), a colour-coded calorie bar per day, and weight history — all rendered in your app language.
🌍
Fully localised export
Every label in the CSV and PDF is translated into all six supported languages: English, German, Greek, Spanish, Italian, and Polish.
🎨
Consistent button styling
The export card, in-modal export button, and Settings logout button now share the same dark forest-green gradient for a cohesive feel.
Aug 27, 2026 v0.5.1 · Build 41 Welcome Drop

First impressions, fixed.

A proper welcome for new users, a streak that actually starts at 1, and a profile redirect that finally works. 🙌

👋
Onboarding guide
New users get a 5-step interactive welcome tour — with real in-app screenshots showing exactly where to tap for meals, the planner, AI generation, streaks, and reminders.
🔥
Streaks start at 1
New accounts no longer show 0 as a streak. Day one is always Day 1.
📅
Annual subscription plan
A yearly plan is now available alongside the monthly and lifetime options — same premium features, better value.
🛠
Profile redirect on setup complete Fix
Tapping Complete in preference setup now reliably lands you on your profile every time. No more blank tab bars or silent failures.
📸
Onboarding images: 85% lighter
In-app screenshots converted from PNG to WebP — 1 MB trimmed to 147 KB. Same clarity, fraction of the weight.
